# SlowSnowTrade 图标

使用内置 imagegen 生成，按用户指定的“简约、卡通、萌趣”风格制作。

最终资产：`../Web/assets/app-icon.png`。构建脚本生成 16–1024 像素的标准 macOS 图标并打包为 `AppIcon.icns`，同时用于应用内品牌标记。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: ONE finished macOS Dock app icon for SlowSnowTrade. User style: simple, cartoon, cute, playful. A plump adorable small white snowman mascot with a round head and squat rounded body, tiny dark dot eyes, a little happy smile, warm pink cheeks, wearing a sky blue scarf and holding a very small charcoal placard bearing three simple sky blue candlestick marks (no letters). Centered on a flat dark charcoal rounded square tile, generous transparent outer margin. Front view. Bold clean soft dark outlines, flat pastel color fills, at most very subtle cel shading. Friendly expressive kawaii illustration with very few large shapes. White and sky blue on near-black, clear silhouette readable as a tiny Dock icon. The snowman should fill most of the tile without crowding the tile edges. No glossy metallic materials, no glass, no neon, no elaborate background, no tiny snow patterns, no lettering, no watermark, no product mockup or scene. Genuinely transparent background outside the rounded square. Create the app icon artwork itself.
